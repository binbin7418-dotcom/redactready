The two-channel leak re-check

Every output is searched twice for every term: (1) the extracted-text layer, and (2) the raw content-stream bytes of every page, plain and hex-encoded — because text can hide where extractors don't look. Zero hits in both channels, or the file does not pass. Pages with no text layer (scans) are flagged SCAN-DETECTED, never passed silently: they need OCR first, and the report tells you so.
